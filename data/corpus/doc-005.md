title: Position - guardrails alone are bypassed by adaptive attacks
source: project-authored summary of a position (replace with real citation)
---
Critics argue that guardrails alone cannot prevent prompt injection. An adaptive attacker who can iterate on the wording can find inputs that a filter misses, and natural language has no fixed grammar that a filter can enumerate. Critics therefore recommend treating filters as one layer and limiting the damage a successful injection can cause.
