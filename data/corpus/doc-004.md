title: Position - filters and guardrails are an effective first layer
source: project-authored summary of a position (replace with real citation)
---
Proponents of filter-based defences argue that guardrails and classifiers reduce the volume of simple injection attempts at low cost. They see filtering as a practical first layer that catches known attack patterns before they reach the model, and argue that dismissing filters would discard a useful control.
